# GenAI Success Canvas

The **GenAI Success Canvas** is a practical tool for structuring and addressing the successful adoption of GenAI from a knowledge management perspective. Instead of treating adoption as a one-off technical project, the canvas helps organizations identify the critical factors that influence success across the whole adoption journey and to build a shared understanding of what needs to be in place at each stage. This enables organizations to discuss GenAI adoption using a common language and to move from isolated experiments toward coordinated, organization-wide implementation.

The canvas is based on the idea that successful GenAI adoption is not only a question of technology, but of how technology, the organization, and people come together as work practices, knowledge, and capabilities evolve. It was developed as part of a thesis examining successful GenAI adoption in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) from a knowledge management perspective. The study applied Design Science Research (DSR) and created the canvas as an artifact, grounded in literature and developed further together with participating companies.

## Structure of the canvas

The canvas organizes the critical factors of GenAI adoption along two complementary axes:

**Implementation phases** – the canvas distinguishes three phases of adoption, recognizing that different factors become critical at different stages:

- **Pre-implementation** – goal setting, readiness, testing and piloting, and preparing the organization and people for change
- **Implementation** – putting the solution into use, training and stakeholder collaboration, and managing the change in practice
- **Post-implementation** – sustaining use, measuring success, gathering feedback, and continuously developing the solution

**Categories** – within each phase, critical factors are grouped into four categories that together provide a holistic view of adoption:

- **Technology** – data quality and quantity, information security, IT infrastructure, suitability of technology, metrics, and managing risks such as data hallucinations
- **Organizational acceptance** – continuous business performance evaluation, testing and piloting, AI teams, stakeholder collaboration, and feedback-driven development
- **Organization** – culture and cultural change, the lifecycle of AI, financial resources, strategy and metrics for monitoring success, ethics, and maturity
- **People** – change management, training, communication, success stories, key users, controlled knowledge creation and transfer, and employee learning

Each **critical factor** is associated with one or more **support questions** that guide reflection and deepen understanding. The canvas can be used on its own to structure adoption, or together with the support questions to assess the critical factors affecting an organization in its specific context.

## How to use the canvas

The canvas supports structured discussion, shared understanding, and decision-making, helping organizations align GenAI with their goals and strategy. A typical way of working with it:

1. **Familiarize yourself with the canvas** and identify which phase of implementation the organization is currently in. Processing can focus on the current phase or encompass all phases.
2. **Review the critical factors systematically**, considering each factor — or focusing first on those most relevant to the organization — and how each is currently addressed.
3. **Use the support questions** associated with each critical factor to guide the discussion and deepen understanding of adoption.
4. **Address the most relevant issues first**, while ensuring that all categories are considered over time, so that the approach to GenAI adoption remains comprehensive.

The canvas is well suited to workshops and other collaborative settings where a shared understanding of GenAI adoption needs to be built across different stakeholders.
