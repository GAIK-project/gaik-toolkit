What about if the practice has a separate CBCT device? Does that bring any additional requirements to this radiation safety training? In terms of legislation, not any more. Previously there was a requirement that certain courses had to be completed, both by the users of the device and by the physician responsible for the procedures, that is, the one who is responsible for those CBCT scans, their justification and so on. But nowadays the legislation no longer has any specific course that is required, but this normal continuing education, radiation safety training, in practice has to include matters related to CBCT training as well, and it is again the responsibility of the operator, the undertaking running the practice, to see that their staff have sufficient competence both in using the device and also in the clinical need for imaging, the interpretation of the image and other related topics.
