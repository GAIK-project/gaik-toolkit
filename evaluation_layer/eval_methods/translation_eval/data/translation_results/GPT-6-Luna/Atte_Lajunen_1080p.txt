What about if there’s a separate CBCT device at the practice? Does that entail any additional requirements for radiation safety training?

Not in terms of legislation anymore. Previously, people had to have completed certain courses, both the users of the device and the doctor responsible for the procedures—that is, the person responsible for those CBCT scans, their justification, and so on. But nowadays, the legislation no longer requires any particular course. However, the usual continuing education, radiation safety training, must in practice also include topics related to CBCT training. And it’s the responsibility of the operator of the practice to ensure that their staff have sufficient competence both in using the device and in assessing the clinical need for imaging, interpreting the images, and other related matters.
