What if the practice has a CBCT unit as well? Does that bring any additional requirements for this radiation safety training?

As far as the legislation is concerned, not anymore. Previously, both the equipment users and the dentist responsible for the procedures, in other words the person responsible for the CBCT examinations, their justification and so on, had to have completed certain courses. But nowadays the legislation no longer requires any specific course. However, in practice, this normal continuing education, the radiation safety training, must also include topics related to CBCT training. And again, it is the operator’s responsibility to make sure their staff have sufficient expertise both in using the equipment and in assessing the clinical need for imaging, interpreting the images and other related matters.
