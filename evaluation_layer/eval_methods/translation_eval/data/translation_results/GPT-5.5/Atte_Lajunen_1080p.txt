Then what if the practice has a CBCT device separately? Does that bring any additional requirements for this radiation protection training?

As such, not in legislative terms anymore. Previously, certain courses had to have been completed both by the users of the device and by the doctor responsible for the procedures, that is, the one who is responsible for the CBCT examinations, their justification, and so on. But nowadays the legislation no longer requires some specific course, but this normal continuing education, radiation safety training, in practice must also include matters related to CBCT training, and it is again the responsibility of the operator to ensure that their staff have sufficient competence both in using the device and also in terms of the clinical need for imaging, image interpretation, and other related topics.
