## Task
Role-aware assistant that answers employee questions from internal policies, equipment manuals, and management documents with citations, enforcing role-based access control so restricted documents are never retrieved or disclosed to unauthorized employees.

Today, an employee who has a question about company policy, equipment maintenance, or a confidential business initiative has to know which document to open and read it themselves. This assistant lets them simply ask the question in plain language and get a direct answer with a pointer to the exact document and page it came from — while automatically keeping commercially sensitive material (like the Project Aurora pricing strategy) visible only to managers, exactly as it is today.

## Business need
**Current process:** Employees manually search across internal policies, equipment manuals, and management documents to find answers to their questions, with no systematic way to verify which source supports an answer.

**Pain points:**
- Manual search across the document collection is slow
- Answers can be incomplete or unsupported because employees may overlook the applicable source

## Proposed solution
An AI assistant that answers employee questions using the document collection, provides citations to the supporting source, and enforces role-based access so restricted documents are never retrieved or disclosed to unauthorized employees.

Instead of manually searching, an employee asks their question once and receives a grounded answer with an exact source citation, eliminating the search-and-skim step and the risk of missing the applicable document. Role-based filtering is applied before any content reaches the AI model, so a question about restricted material is refused cleanly rather than answered from a document the employee should never see — turning "search everywhere and hope" into "ask and get a trustworthy, permission-aware answer."

## Users and stakeholders
- **Intended users:** employee, manager
- **Reviewers:** _none_
- **Stakeholders:** knowledge owners (maintain and classify the source documents), managers (authorized to access management-confidential material), employees (primary users of the assistant)

## Knowledge object
- **Input artifacts:** internal policies, equipment manuals, commercially sensitive management documents
- **Target output artifacts:** role-aware answer to an employee question, grounded with citations to the supporting source

The central knowledge object is the **role-aware answer record**: a single employee question is transformed into a grounded answer plus its supporting `[document, page]` citations, filtered end-to-end through the requester's role so the transformation never touches content that role is not permitted to see.

## Expected benefits
- faster access to reliable internal knowledge
- traceable answers via citations
- enforced access boundaries that prevent unauthorized disclosure

## Success criteria
- Employees find reliable answers faster, can verify answers through citations, and never receive content outside their role. No numerical time-saving, accuracy, cost, or adoption target was specified.

## Evaluation focus
Deterministic acceptance test using the four supplied role-tagged queries (Q01-Q04), checked against required facts, the exact access decision (allowed/denied), and required citation pairs per query. No numerical RAG-quality threshold (e.g. a fixed recall/precision score) was specified for the first PoC.

## Risks and constraints
- hallucinated answers
- fabricated or incorrect citations
- retrieval of restricted chunks into the model context
- leakage of restricted content through answer text or citations
- stale or outdated source documents
- returning an answer when the authorized collection contains no support
