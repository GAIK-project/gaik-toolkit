## Task
Role-aware question-answering assistant that retrieves cited answers from internal manufacturing policies, equipment manuals, and management documents, with pre-retrieval RBAC to enforce document access boundaries.

Instead of manually searching folders, employees type a plain-language question and receive an answer in seconds — grounded in approved internal documents and linked to the exact source page. Role-based access rules ensure that management-confidential material is only surfaced to authorised users, enforcing the same boundaries that exist on the documents themselves.

## Business need
**Current process:** Employees manually search internal policies, equipment manuals, and management documents to find answers; the process is slow and may result in incomplete or unsupported responses.

**Pain points:**
- Manual document search is time-consuming
- Employees may miss the most relevant or applicable source
- No enforcement of document access controls during informal searches
- Answers are not traceable to a verified source

## Proposed solution
A RAG-based assistant that accepts natural-language questions, applies pre-retrieval RBAC based on the requestor role, retrieves semantically relevant chunks from allowed documents only, and generates cited answers in a structured RAGAnswerRecord format.

The assistant replaces scattered manual searches with a single query interface. Semantic retrieval surfaces the most relevant passages regardless of which document they sit in, and the pre-retrieval access filter ensures the system never returns — or even processes — content the requestor is not permitted to see. Every factual answer includes page-level citations so users can verify the source without opening a document.

## Users and stakeholders
- **Intended users:** employee, manager, knowledge_owner
- **Reviewers:** _none_
- **Stakeholders:** knowledge_owners, managers, IT

## Knowledge object
- **Input artifacts:** employee_travel_policy.pdf, mx200_maintenance_manual.pdf, project_aurora_pricing_strategy.pdf, access_manifest.json, query_set.json, poc_input_bundle.json
- **Target output artifacts:** RAGAnswerRecord JSON list (query_id, role, question, access_decision, answer, citations, refusal_reason)

The central knowledge object is the company's internal document corpus (policies, manuals, strategy papers); the system transforms it into a role-filtered, searchable vector index and returns structured answer records with page-level source citations for each employee query.

## Expected benefits
- Employees find reliable answers faster
- Every factual answer is traceable to a verified source via citations
- Restricted documents are never disclosed to unauthorized roles
- Reduced risk of incomplete or unsupported answers

## Success criteria
- Q01 (employee, Helsinki hotel limit): answers EUR 180 per night with Finance Director approval required when exceeded, citing [employee_travel_policy.pdf, 3]
- Q02 (employee, MX-200 filter): answers inspection every 250 operating hours and replacement after 1,000 operating hours or 1.8 bar, citing mx200_maintenance_manual.pdf pages 3 and 4
- Q03 (employee, Project Aurora ceiling): access_decision=denied, empty citations list, no restricted facts disclosed
- Q04 (manager, Project Aurora ceiling and approver): answers 12 percent ceiling with written CFO approval required above the ceiling, citing [project_aurora_pricing_strategy.pdf, 3]
- All four results returned as separate non-empty parseable JSON objects in output/results.json

## Risks and constraints
- Hallucinated or speculative answers not grounded in retrieved content
- Fabricated or incorrect citations
- Unauthorized chunk leakage into model context via pre-filter bypass
- Restricted facts disclosed in answer text on a denied query
- Stale documents producing outdated answers
- Answer returned when no authorized source supports the claim
