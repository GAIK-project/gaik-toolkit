## Task
Combine meeting audio recordings, agenda documents, and participant lists into a structured, evidence-cited meeting record capturing decisions, actions, and unresolved issues.

Today, preparing a meeting record means a coordinator manually re-listening to a recording while cross-checking the agenda and attendee list — slow, and easy to get inconsistent from meeting to meeting. This solution automates that preparation step: it listens to the recording, reads the agenda, and produces a first-draft record the project manager can review and approve in minutes instead of the coordinator spending an hour writing it from scratch.

## Business need
**Current process:** A project coordinator listens to the meeting recording, checks the agenda and participant list, and manually prepares the meeting record.

**Pain points:**
- Manual preparation takes considerable time
- Action tracking is inconsistent across meetings
- Decisions are difficult to trace back to their source (recording or agenda)

## Proposed solution
An AI-supported pipeline that combines the meeting recording, agenda document, and participant list to produce a reviewable, structured meeting record that separates agenda topics, discussion summaries, decisions, assigned actions, unresolved issues, and conflicts, each backed by source citations.

This directly targets the three pain points above: transcription and extraction replace the manual listen-and-type work, a fixed schema with an owner/due-date field per action makes tracking consistent across meetings, and every decision, action, unresolved issue, and conflict carries a citation back to the exact audio timestamp or agenda page it came from — so nothing has to be taken on trust. The pipeline is deliberately conservative: it leaves a field unset rather than guessing, so the project manager's review is a check on genuinely uncertain items, not a search for fabricated ones.
