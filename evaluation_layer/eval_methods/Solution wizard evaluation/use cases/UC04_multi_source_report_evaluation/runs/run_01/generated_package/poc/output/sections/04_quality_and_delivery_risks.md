## Quality and Delivery Risks

- **Nordic Components:** Nordic Components is the clearest supported Q2 quality and delivery risk in the current supplier group. It recorded 85.2% on-time delivery and a 3.1% defective-unit rate, and it remained on conditional status throughout the quarter [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx]. Delivery risk is supported by three recorded incidents, including INC-052 and INC-063, which caused a 6-hour and 10-hour assembly delay respectively, while INC-041 caused a 2-day delay without production stoppage [delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. Quality risk is supported by the 18 June audit score of 72/100, two major nonconformities for missing calibration records on 4 of 10 sampled gauges and overdue CAPA-17, and a minor packaging-label traceability issue [nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf]. The meeting discussion referred to spend as approximately EUR 410,000, but the authoritative workbook records exact Q2 spend of EUR 405,000 and that exact figure should be used in the report [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx]. No order-volume increase is permitted until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85/100 [nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md].

- **Baltic Fasteners:** The available evidence supports a low current Q2 quality and delivery risk position for Baltic Fasteners. The supplier remained approved, achieved 96.9% on-time delivery, and recorded a 1.0% defective-unit rate, with no formal Q2 corrective action in the quality review [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx]. Delivery detail shows one late machine-screw delivery event in each month, but no linked incident reference is recorded, and the meeting notes describe overall delivery and quality performance as stable [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. On the evidence provided, routine monthly monitoring is the supported response rather than escalation [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx].

- **Alpine Sensors:** The supported risk for Alpine Sensors is supply continuity rather than current-quarter delivery or quality failure. Q2 performance was acceptable, with 97.0% on-time delivery, a 0.6% defective-unit rate, and approved status [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. The only recorded incident, INC-058, was a 1-day weather-related delay affecting temperature probes with no production impact, and both the incident register and meeting notes state that it should be treated as a weather disruption rather than a supplier-quality failure [delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. Management’s stated concern is continued single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items, so the agreed response is ongoing supply-continuity monitoring rather than formal corrective action or supplier-status restriction [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md].
