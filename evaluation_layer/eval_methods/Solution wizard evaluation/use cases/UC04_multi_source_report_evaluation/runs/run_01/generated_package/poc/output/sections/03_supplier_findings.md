## Supplier Findings

- **Nordic Components:** Nordic Components was the weakest Q2 performer in the supplier group on both delivery and quality, with 135 deliveries, 85.2% on-time delivery, a 3.1% defective-unit rate, and EUR 405,000 spend. The workbook and meeting record both place the supplier on conditional status, and the meeting notes clarify that an approximate spend of EUR 410,000 was discussed but the authoritative workbook value is EUR 405,000. Three delivery incidents were recorded: INC-041 caused a 2-day delay with no production stoppage, INC-052 caused a 4-day delay and a 6-hour assembly delay, and INC-063 caused a 5-day delay and a 10-hour assembly delay. The 18 June audit scored 72/100 and identified two major nonconformities, missing calibration records for 4 of 10 sampled gauges and overdue CAPA-17, plus a minor packaging-label traceability issue. Management agreed that the supplier remains active but no order-volume increase is permitted until the corrective-action plan is accepted and a follow-up audit achieves at least 85/100 [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv].

- **Baltic Fasteners:** Baltic Fasteners delivered stable Q2 performance, with 97 deliveries, 96.9% on-time delivery, a 1.0% defective-unit rate, and EUR 194,000 spend. The workbook shows approved status throughout the quarter, accepted-after-sampling defect disposition, and no formal Q2 corrective action in the quality review. Delivery detail shows one late machine-screw delivery event in each month, but no linked incident reference is recorded, and the meeting notes state that delivery and quality performance remained stable overall. On that basis, procurement agreed that Baltic Fasteners is the preferred source for incremental fastener demand in the next quarter, with the meeting record stating that this decision is limited to additional fastener demand rather than general supplier replacement [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md].

- **Alpine Sensors:** Alpine Sensors delivered acceptable Q2 results, with 67 deliveries, 97.0% on-time delivery, a 0.6% defective-unit rate, and EUR 268,000 spend. The supplier remained approved in the workbook, and the quality review and meeting notes both state that Q2 delivery and quality performance was acceptable. The only recorded delivery incident was INC-058, a 1-day weather-related delay affecting temperature probes; the incident register and meeting notes both state that it had no production impact and should be treated as a weather disruption rather than a supplier-quality failure. The main management concern is therefore not current-quarter execution but continuing single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items, which procurement agreed should remain under ongoing supply-continuity monitoring rather than formal corrective action or supplier-status restriction [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv].
