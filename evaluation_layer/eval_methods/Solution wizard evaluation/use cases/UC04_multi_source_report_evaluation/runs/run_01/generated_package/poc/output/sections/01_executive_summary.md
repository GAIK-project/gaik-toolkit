## Executive Summary
Q2 supplier performance was mixed at portfolio level [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx] [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. Across all suppliers, the business recorded 299 deliveries, 274 of them on time, with total spend of EUR 867,000 and an overall defect rate of 2.0% [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx]. Baltic Fasteners and Alpine Sensors remained on approved status, with Baltic Fasteners identified as the preferred source for incremental fastener demand in the next quarter, while Alpine Sensors' Q2 delivery and quality performance was considered acceptable despite ongoing single-source lead-time exposure [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx] [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md].

The main concern in Q2 was Nordic Components [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx] [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. It remained on conditional status after recording weaker delivery and quality results than the other suppliers, including 115 on-time deliveries out of 135, 415 defective units out of 13,500 received, and Q2 spend of EUR 405,000, which is the authoritative value and overrides the approximate EUR 410,000 discussed in the meeting [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx] [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]. The supplier also received an audit score of 72 / 100 with major findings on missing calibration records and overdue CAPA-17, and two delivery incidents caused assembly delays of 6 hours and 10 hours [nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf] [delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv]. Management decided that Nordic Components remains active under conditional monitoring, but no order-volume increase is permitted until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85 / 100 [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md] [nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf].
