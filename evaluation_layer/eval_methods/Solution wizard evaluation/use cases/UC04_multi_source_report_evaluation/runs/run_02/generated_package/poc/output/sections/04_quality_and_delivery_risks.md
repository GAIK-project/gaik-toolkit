## Quality and Delivery Risks

- **Nordic Components.** Nordic Components is the clearest Q2 quality and delivery risk in the supplier group. Its Q2 results were 85.2% on-time delivery and 3.1% defective units, with three recorded delivery incidents and two incidents that affected assembly: INC-052 caused a 6-hour assembly delay and INC-063 caused a 10-hour assembly delay. Quality controls also remain a concern. The 18 June 2026 audit scored the supplier at 72/100 and kept it in Conditional status, with two major findings on missing calibration records for 4 of 10 sampled gauges and overdue CAPA-17, plus a minor packaging-label traceability issue affecting 3 of 20 reviewed shipments. The meeting discussion referred to approximately EUR 410,000 of Q2 spend, but the KPI workbook is authoritative for exact spend and records EUR 405,000, which is the figure that should be used in the report. Management agreed that the supplier remains active, but order volume must not be increased until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85/100. - **Baltic Fasteners.** Baltic Fasteners does not present a supported major Q2 quality or delivery risk. It remained in Approved status, recorded 96.9% on-time delivery and a 1.0% defect rate, and the quality review states that no formal Q2 corrective action is required. The main watchpoint is narrower than for Nordic Components: the delivery detail shows one late machine-screws delivery event in each month of the quarter. Based on the available evidence, this supports continued routine monitoring rather than escalation. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]

- **Alpine Sensors.** Alpine Sensors also does not show a supported current Q2 quality or delivery failure that would justify corrective action or supplier-status restriction. It remained Approved, recorded 97.0% on-time delivery and a 0.6% defect rate, and its only logged incident was INC-058, a 1-day weather-related delay on temperature probes with no production impact. The supported risk is instead single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items, so the appropriate risk statement is supply-continuity monitoring rather than escalation of Q2 delivery or quality performance.
