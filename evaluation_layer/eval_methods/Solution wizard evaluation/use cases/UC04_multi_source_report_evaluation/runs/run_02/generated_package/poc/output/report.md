# Q2 2026 Supplier Performance Report

## Executive Summary
Q2 supplier performance was mixed at portfolio level. Across all suppliers, the business recorded 299 deliveries, 274 of them on time, with total Q2 spend of EUR 867,000. Baltic Fasteners and Alpine Sensors both remained on approved status with stronger delivery and quality results, while Nordic Components was the clear underperformer in the quarter [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md].

The main concern is Nordic Components. It closed Q2 on conditional status with 135 deliveries, 115 on time, 415 defective units, and EUR 405,000 in spend, and the Q2 audit scored the supplier at 72/100. The audit identified two major nonconformities, incomplete calibration records and overdue CAPA-17, plus a packaging-label traceability issue. Three delivery incidents were recorded for Nordic Components in Q2, and two of those incidents caused assembly delays [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv].

Management decided to keep Nordic Components active under conditional monitoring and not increase its order volume until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85/100. In parallel, Baltic Fasteners was agreed as the preferred source for incremental fastener demand in the next quarter, while Alpine Sensors remains acceptable but subject to continued monitoring of single-source lead-time exposure [procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx].

## Supplier Findings

- **Nordic Components.** Nordic Components had the weakest Q2 result in the supplier group, with 135 deliveries, 85.2% on-time delivery, 13,500 units received, 3.1% defective units, and exact spend of EUR 405,000. The meeting discussion referred to approximately EUR 410,000, but the KPI workbook is the authoritative source for exact spend and the report uses EUR 405,000. Three delivery incidents were recorded in Q2, and two of them affected assembly: INC-052 caused a 6-hour assembly delay and INC-063 caused a 10-hour assembly delay. The 18 June 2026 audit scored the supplier at 72/100 and confirmed conditional status, with major findings on missing calibration records for 4 of 10 sampled gauges and overdue CAPA-17, plus a minor shipment-label traceability issue affecting 3 of 20 reviewed shipments. Management agreed that the supplier remains active, but order volume must not be increased until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85/100. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv]

- **Baltic Fasteners.** Baltic Fasteners showed stable Q2 performance, with 97 deliveries, 96.9% on-time delivery, 9,700 units received, 1.0% defective units, and spend of EUR 194,000. Its workbook status is Approved, and the quality review states that no formal Q2 corrective action is required. The delivery detail shows one late machine-screws delivery event in each month, but overall delivery and quality performance remained materially stronger than Nordic Components. Based on this result, management agreed that Baltic Fasteners should be the preferred source for incremental fastener demand in the next quarter. The meeting notes also state that this decision applies only to additional fastener demand and should not be presented as a general replacement of other suppliers. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]

- **Alpine Sensors.** Alpine Sensors delivered acceptable Q2 performance, with 67 deliveries, 97.0% on-time delivery, 3,350 units received, 0.6% defective units, and spend of EUR 268,000. The only recorded delivery incident was INC-058, a 1-day weather-related delay on temperature probes, and it had no production impact. The supplier remained in Approved status for Q2, and the meeting notes are clear that no corrective action or supplier-status restriction was approved. The main management concern is instead single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items, so the agreed response is continued monitoring of supply continuity and recovery lead time rather than escalation of current Q2 performance. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv]

## Quality and Delivery Risks

- **Nordic Components.** Nordic Components is the clearest Q2 quality and delivery risk in the supplier group. Its Q2 results were 85.2% on-time delivery and 3.1% defective units, with three recorded delivery incidents and two incidents that affected assembly: INC-052 caused a 6-hour assembly delay and INC-063 caused a 10-hour assembly delay. Quality controls also remain a concern. The 18 June 2026 audit scored the supplier at 72/100 and kept it in Conditional status, with two major findings on missing calibration records for 4 of 10 sampled gauges and overdue CAPA-17, plus a minor packaging-label traceability issue affecting 3 of 20 reviewed shipments. The meeting discussion referred to approximately EUR 410,000 of Q2 spend, but the KPI workbook is authoritative for exact spend and records EUR 405,000, which is the figure that should be used in the report. Management agreed that the supplier remains active, but order volume must not be increased until the corrective-action plan is accepted and the follow-up audit score is at least 85/100. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; nordic_components_quality_audit.pdf; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv]

- **Baltic Fasteners.** Baltic Fasteners does not present a supported major Q2 quality or delivery risk. It remained in Approved status, recorded 96.9% on-time delivery and a 1.0% defect rate, and the quality review states that no formal Q2 corrective action is required. The main watchpoint is narrower than for Nordic Components: the delivery detail shows one late machine-screws delivery event in each month of the quarter. Based on the available evidence, this supports continued routine monitoring rather than escalation. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md]

- **Alpine Sensors.** Alpine Sensors also does not show a supported current Q2 quality or delivery failure that would justify corrective action or supplier-status restriction. It remained Approved, recorded 97.0% on-time delivery and a 0.6% defect rate, and its only logged incident was INC-058, a 1-day weather-related delay on temperature probes with no production impact. The supported risk is instead single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items, so the appropriate risk statement is supply-continuity monitoring rather than escalation of Q2 delivery or quality performance. [supplier_kpis_q2_2026.xlsx; procurement_meeting_notes_q2_2026.md; delivery_incidents_q2_2026.csv]
