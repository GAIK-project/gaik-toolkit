## Portfolio-level discussion

The overall portfolio recorded 299 deliveries, of which 274 were on time. Total Q2 spend was EUR 867,000. The group agreed that the aggregate result should not hide the performance difference between Nordic Components and the other two suppliers. Nordic Components requires management attention because both its delivery performance and incoming-quality results are weaker than those of Baltic Fasteners and Alpine Sensors.

The report should present percentages rounded to one decimal place. Exact spend must come from the KPI workbook. Where a meeting note uses an approximate value, the report should state the discrepancy and use the workbook value for the final calculation.

## Supplier review: Nordic Components

### Delivery and quality observations

- Nordic Components recorded 135 deliveries in Q2, with 115 received on time.
- Three delivery incidents were recorded during the quarter. Two of the incidents caused assembly delays.
- The 18 June 2026 quality audit resulted in a score of 72 / 100 and a conditional status.
- The audit identified missing calibration records for 4 of 10 sampled gauges.
- Corrective action CAPA-17 was overdue from 31 May 2026.
- Three of 20 reviewed shipments had packaging labels without a batch number.
- The quarterly spend was described during discussion as approximately EUR 410,000. The exact value in the KPI workbook is EUR 405,000 and must be used in the report.

### Management position

Nordic Components remains on conditional supplier status. Do not increase its order volume until both of the following conditions are met:

1. the corrective-action plan has been accepted; and
2. the follow-up audit score is at least 85 / 100.

The procurement manager asked the report author to distinguish between the current conditional status and supplier rejection. The supplier remains active, but it is subject to additional monitoring and no automatic volume increase is allowed.

## Supplier review: Baltic Fasteners

Baltic Fasteners showed stable delivery and quality performance throughout Q2. Its on-time delivery rate was materially higher than Nordic Components' rate, and the incoming defect rate remained controlled. No formal corrective action is required for the quarter.

Baltic Fasteners is the preferred source for incremental fastener demand in the next quarter. This decision applies to additional fastener demand only and should not be presented as a general replacement of other suppliers.

## Supplier review: Alpine Sensors

Alpine Sensors' Q2 delivery and quality performance is acceptable. One weather-related delay was recorded, but it did not interrupt production. The supplier's main management concern is not Q2 quality performance; it is single-source lead-time exposure for critical sensor items.

The report should recommend continued supply-continuity monitoring. It should not claim that a corrective action or supplier-status restriction was approved for Alpine Sensors.

## Report drafting guidance

The report must contain the agreed sections: Executive Summary, KPI Overview, Supplier Findings, Quality and Delivery Risks, Actions and Owners, and Source References. Material factual claims should cite the exact source filename in square brackets. The report should clearly separate measured facts from management decisions and should not invent an action, owner, deadline, supplier, or market condition.

For Nordic Components, the report should explain the spend discrepancy rather than silently choosing the approximate meeting-note value. It should state that the meeting used approximately EUR 410,000 while the authoritative workbook records EUR 405,000.

## Review and return workflow

The procurement analyst prepares the first draft. The procurement manager checks:

- required report sections and title;
- calculations and rounded KPI values;
- exact supplier names, actions, owners, and due dates;
- treatment of the Nordic spend discrepancy;
- completeness and accuracy of source references; and
- absence of unsupported claims.

If the manager returns the draft, the analyst corrects the source data or report instructions and regenerates the report. The corrected draft returns to the same manager-review step. Only an approved report may be released or distributed.
