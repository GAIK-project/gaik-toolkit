## Task
Field technicians report facility faults via Finnish voice messages; the system transcribes, extracts a structured maintenance ticket, validates it, and presents it to a supervisor for approval before entry into the maintenance system.

Instead of typing fault details into a form, a field technician records a short Finnish voice message describing what is broken, where it is, and how urgent it is. The AI system listens to the recording and automatically fills in a structured maintenance ticket, which a supervisor then reviews and approves before it reaches the maintenance management system.

## Business need
**Current process:** Field technicians manually enter fault information (what is broken, where it is, urgency) into the maintenance system using a computer or mobile form.

**Pain points:**
- Manual entry is slow, especially in the field
- Details are frequently omitted or incorrectly recorded
- No structured validation before tickets reach the maintenance system

## Proposed solution
An AI workflow that accepts a Finnish voice message from a technician, transcribes it, extracts a structured maintenance ticket, validates the extraction, and routes the ticket to a supervisor for approval before it is entered into the maintenance system.

The solution replaces manual form entry with a voice-first workflow: the technician speaks naturally in Finnish, and the AI extracts all required ticket fields — location, fault description, and urgency — automatically. An LLM judge validates the extraction before it reaches the supervisor, catching missing or inconsistent values early. The mandatory supervisor approval gate ensures that no ticket enters the maintenance system without a human sign-off, directly mitigating the risk of unauthorised or incorrect transfers.

## Users and stakeholders
- **Intended users:** field_technician
- **Reviewers:** supervisor
- **Stakeholders:** _none_

## Knowledge object
- **Input artifacts:** Finnish voice message recorded by the field technician via mobile app
- **Target output artifacts:** Structured maintenance ticket (JSON) approved by supervisor

The central knowledge object is the **MaintenanceTicket**: a Finnish voice recording is transcribed and transformed into a structured JSON ticket containing the fault location, description, urgency level, and optional metadata such as asset identifier, observation time, actions taken, and a list of any fields that could not be reliably extracted.

## Expected benefits
- Reduced manual data entry time for technicians
- Fewer missing or incorrect details in maintenance tickets
- Faster fault response through structured, supervisor-approved tickets

## Success criteria
- Faster incident reporting with fewer missing details
- Supervisor can verify and approve each ticket before it enters the maintenance system
