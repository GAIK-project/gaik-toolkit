## Task
Convert Finnish voice messages from field technicians into structured maintenance tickets for supervisor review and system entry.

Instead of typing fault details into a computer, a field technician records a short voice message on their mobile phone describing what is broken, where it is, and how urgent the repair is. The AI system converts that spoken Finnish report into a structured digital ticket, which a supervisor then reviews and approves before it is entered into the maintenance management system.

## Business need
**Current process:** Field technicians manually enter fault reports into the maintenance system at a computer, describing the fault, location, and urgency by hand.

**Pain points:**
- Manual data entry is slow, especially in the field
- Tickets often have missing or incomplete details
- No structured review gate before system entry

## Proposed solution
An AI workflow that accepts Finnish voice messages from technicians, transcribes and extracts structured ticket fields, and routes the ticket to a supervisor for approval before system entry.

The solution removes manual typing entirely: a technician speaks a fault report in Finnish and the pipeline transcribes, structures, and validates it automatically. Required fields that were not mentioned are flagged as uncertain rather than silently omitted, so incomplete tickets are visible before they reach the supervisor. A mandatory approval gate ensures that no ticket can reach the maintenance system without explicit supervisor sign-off.

## Users and stakeholders
- **Intended users:** field_technician
- **Reviewers:** supervisor
- **Stakeholders:** field_technician, supervisor

## Knowledge object
- **Input artifacts:** Finnish voice message (MP3, M4A, or WAV) recorded on a mobile device
- **Target output artifacts:** Structured maintenance ticket (JSON) with supervisor approval decision

The central knowledge object is the **maintenance ticket** — a spoken fault observation that the pipeline transforms from an unstructured Finnish voice message into a validated, structured JSON record containing the fault location, description, urgency, and supporting details, ready for supervisor review.

## Expected benefits
- Reduced manual data-entry effort for field technicians
- Higher ticket completeness and field accuracy
- Structured approval gate eliminates unauthorised system transfers

## Success criteria
- Faster incident reporting compared to manual entry
- Fewer missing details in submitted tickets
- Every ticket is supervisor-verifiable before transfer to the maintenance system
