## Overview

Automatically generated reports synthesize information from multiple sources into professional documents. Evaluation methods assess completeness, coherence, accuracy, and professional quality of generated reports.

### What Report Writing Evaluation Measures

**Content Quality**
- Are all required sections included and properly structured?
- Is the information accurate and consistent with source materials?
- Are key findings and recommendations clearly presented?

**Writing Quality**
- Is the report professionally written and grammatically correct?
- Does the content flow logically from section to section?
- Is technical terminology used appropriately?

**Completeness**
- Does the report address all specified requirements?
- Are citations and references properly included?
- Is supporting data and evidence adequately presented?
