description: Automated purchase order intake, validation, and structured extraction

Automates purchase order intake, validation, and structured extraction for downstream ERP and approval workflows. The system reads purchase orders and bills of material, matches items with pricing data, applies business rules, and generates structured sales orders ready for processing.
