---
title: Construction Site Diary Creation
description: Automated daily site documentation from voice notes and field observations
---

Generates standardized daily site diaries from voice notes, images, and field observations. Field workers record daily activities via voice or text, and the system extracts structured information about tasks completed, materials used, weather conditions, issues encountered, and safety observations.
