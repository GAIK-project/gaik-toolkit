# Kaarna Cycle Workshop: customer FAQ

Kaarna Cycle Workshop is a fictional bicycle repair shop, written as sample data for the GAIK Helpdesk Chatbot demo.

## How do I book a service?

Book a time on the booking page or at the counter. Bring the bike in on the morning of the booked day before 10. From April to June the queue for a service is about one week; at other times a service starts within two working days.

## What are your opening hours?

The workshop is open Monday to Friday from 9 to 18 and on Saturday from 10 to 15. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Bikes can be picked up until closing time.

## Can I get a bike while mine is being serviced?

Yes. Service customers can rent a city bike for the days their own bike is in the workshop. There are six replacement bikes, so reserve one when you book the service.

## What does the warranty on a repair cover?

Every repair has a six-month warranty on the work and a twelve-month warranty on the parts we fitted. The warranty does not cover normal wear, such as worn brake pads or tyres, or damage from a crash.

## What if the repair costs more than the estimate?

We call or text you before doing any work that raises the price by more than 20 euros over the estimate. Nothing extra is done without your approval, and you can always take the bike back with only the agreed work done.

## Do you service electric bikes?

Yes, all common mid-drive and hub-motor systems. An e-bike service includes a battery health check and a software update. We do not open battery packs; a faulty battery is sent to the manufacturer's service, which takes two to three weeks.

## How can I pay?

By card, mobile payment or invoice at pick-up. Companies with a service agreement are invoiced once a month. We do not take cash.

## How do I return something I bought in the shop?

Unused parts and accessories can be returned within 30 days with the receipt. Fitted parts, cut cables and opened chemicals cannot be returned. Faulty products are replaced or refunded.

## How long do you keep a finished bike?

Pick the bike up within 14 days of our message that it is ready. After that, storage costs 2 euros a day. Winter storage is a separate service.
