Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retrieval step with a language model to answer questions grounded in a specific document corpus. When a user submits a query, the retrieval component searches a vector index and returns the most relevant text chunks. These chunks are then passed to the language model as context, allowing it to generate an answer that is factually grounded in the source documents rather than relying solely on parametric knowledge. The quality of the final answer depends critically on how well the retrieval step surfaces the right information: if relevant passages are missing or ranked too low, the model cannot produce a correct response regardless of its generation capability.
